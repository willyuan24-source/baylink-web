  (`ggb-deck-mid` for the bridge, `osm-w32776540` for Sutro Baths), whose names the fixes correct; the curated
  `sutro-baths` dot is hidden (kept in places.json). The **Golden Gate Bridge badge stands on the south tower**: at the
  phone's whole-city fit the mid-span point sits 7 px from the frame edge and lost its label; on the tower it is labelled.
- **Categories** (the lead's mapping, with care): university → campus; shopping / food-market → shopping (but the Ferry
  Building T1 stays a landmark); religious → culture + Church glyph; beaches / islands / piers → coast (Sailboat for
  Alcatraz, Treasure Island, PIER 39, ships); the zoo and the bison → park + PawPrint; stages → culture + Theater;
  the Palace, Fort Point, Haas-Lilienthal, Palace Hotel → Castle; lookouts → Binoculars.
- **T2 = 48** (34 JSON + 7 modelled landmarks + 7 famous curated places), inside the plan's "≈ 45".
- **offWalk only for the islands** (Alcatraz → the Pier 33 telescope, Treasure Island → the Pier 14 telescope). The Bay
  Bridge's row (the SF anchorage on Rincon Hill) is on the walking graph, so it needs none.
- **Flags**: placeholders until lane L; `withSiteFlags(list, siteFlagTop)` already takes L's poles for 27 of 64 (tested
  against L's real function).
- **The SF-land fit box** must be x −900…1100, z −100…1860 (not the plan's …1000 / …1760): Fort Funston (z 1842) and
  Candlestick Point (x 1094) are attractions.
- **Extra rows stay runtime** (`extraPlaces.ts`), as the frozen `sf-data` pins require; their snaps are generated by the
  same sidecar that rebuilds places.json and verified by `--check`.
- **Components import no CSS** (node renders them in tests); CityMap imports `map-w4.css` once.
- **Search**: category words score 0.5 (below an exact alias), so "大学" lists every campus and the owner's SF State wins
  on its T1 bonus; lines get the T1 bonus, so "N 线" / "muni" answer with lines first.
