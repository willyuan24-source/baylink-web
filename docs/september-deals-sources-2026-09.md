## Ongoing and birthday benefits

- **IKEA Family:** ordinary coffee or tea without an additional purchase. Official general pages describe a visit benefit, while a separate help page mentions weekdays/participating locations. The article explicitly asks readers to confirm weekends and restaurant hours, and does not promise all-day or weekend availability. [Family benefits](https://www.ikea.com/us/en/ikea-family/), [help page](https://www.ikea.com/us/en/customer-service/knowledge/articles/f26bebdc-91f3-488c-927e-8e8631e9cc48.html).
- **Sephora Beauty Insider:** birthday month or two weeks before/after the birthday; once per year; in-store redemption needs no purchase. Redemption on Sephora's own website requires $25 in merchandise after discounts, before tax; this minimum does not apply to Kohls.com. Gifts vary by availability and tier. [2026 birthday gifts](https://www.sephora.com/beauty/birthday-gift), [membership terms](https://www.sephora.com/beauty/terms-conditions-beauty-insider).
- **Peet’s birthday reward:** enroll at least seven days beforehand and provide birthday at registration; at least one qualifying scanned or Mobile Order Ahead purchase in the previous 180 days. Birthday voucher valid for 14 days; activate Birthday Bonus at redemption; bottled drinks excluded. This is a past-purchase qualification, not an unconditional new-member free drink. [FAQ](https://faq.peets.com/hc/en-us/articles/14985828428052-Will-I-receive-a-Birthday-Reward), [terms](https://www.peets.com/pages/peetnik-rewards-terms-and-conditions).

Individual location pages verify addresses, not participation or inventory. No telephone confirmation was made. The article distinguishes four dated offers from three ongoing/conditional benefits and switches its edition notice to an archive warning after September in Los Angeles time.
