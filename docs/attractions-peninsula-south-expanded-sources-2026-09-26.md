# Peninsula and South Bay attraction expansion

Editorial verification date: 2026-09-26 (America/Los_Angeles). Four evergreen guides and four attraction cards. No monthly edition. Route order, suggested duration, observation prompts and decisions to shorten a visit are editorial suggestions, not operator promises.

## San Carlos: Hiller Aviation Museum

- [General information](https://www.hiller.org/visit/general-information/) — successfully opened. Supports admission requirement, free museum parking, address, last walking segment from San Carlos Caltrain, wheelchair access and mezzanine elevator. It warns that private events may change opening.
- [Exhibits](https://www.hiller.org/museum/exhibits/) — successfully opened through the museum navigation; a repeat request returned 403. The official indexed page was also read and described early flight, airliner cockpit/nose exhibits and aviation stories. No aircraft count, guaranteed cockpit access or daily activity promise is carried into the guide.
- [Visitor guide](https://www.hiller.org/visitors-guide/) — successfully opened. Provides the operator's exhibit-navigation resource; the map is not represented as a guarantee that every exhibit is available today.

The first three route stops intentionally share the museum destination: they are stages of an indoor visit rather than invented standalone map locations. Airport runways and restricted operating areas are excluded. The general-information page and homepage showed different dated private-event notices, so the guide uses the live calendar as the departure-day check and does not freeze exact hours or fees.

## San Mateo: Coyote Point

- [Park overview](https://www.smcgov.org/parks/coyote-point-recreation-area) — successfully opened. Supports promenade, marina and separately operated CuriOdyssey destinations.
- [Activities](https://www.smcgov.org/parks/coyote-point-recreation-area-activities) — successfully opened. Supports promenade improvements, beach access, wildlife/aircraft viewing, vehicle entry fee and restricted scope of CuriOdyssey member parking privileges.
- [Current closures](https://www.smcgov.org/parks/coyote-point-closures) — successfully opened. September 15, 2026 notice says Magic Mountain Playground, nearby picnic sites, adjacent parking and EV chargers are closed for construction beginning September 14. This overrides older promotional playground descriptions still visible on the activities page. The guide's dated warning makes no reopening guarantee.
- [Construction announcement](https://www.smcgov.org/parks/news/construction-begins-new-magic-mountain-playground-sept-14) — successfully opened as a corroborating source. An expected spring 2027 reopening is not treated as a confirmed availability date.
- [CuriOdyssey hours and admission](https://curiodyssey.org/visit/hours-admission/) — successfully opened via `/visit/` redirect. Confirms separate museum admission and park vehicle fee.

Cost is mixed because the featured outing has outdoor park areas plus optional museum admission. Vehicle entry, rentals and classes are separate costs. The route does not depend on a playground being open and does not recommend water entry.

## San Jose: Rosicrucian Egyptian Museum and Municipal Rose Garden

- [Admissions and hours](https://egyptianmuseum.org/admissions-hours) — successfully opened. Supports separate museum admission, limited opening days, ticket conditions and current park-construction notice. Exact hours and fees are left to the operator.
- [Accessibility](https://www.egyptianmuseum.org/accessibility) — successfully opened. Confirms entry ramp, multilevel galleries, wheelchair platform lift and the explicit prohibition on using that lift for strollers. Dimensions and assistance must be checked with the museum for the individual visitor.
- [Directions](https://egyptianmuseum.org/directions) — successfully opened. Supports 1660 Park Avenue entrance and separate parking locations. Listed bus route numbers may age, so no number is copied; riders are directed to check current VTA service.
- [Municipal Rose Garden](https://www.sanjoseca.gov/Home/Components/FacilityDirectory/FacilityDirectory/2295/2051?npage=3) — detailed official search-index text was read; direct open returned 403. Supports Naglee/Dana location, ordinary free visits without reservation, designated paths and seasonal flowers. No exact flower count, peak-bloom guarantee or fixed hours appear in the guide.
- [Museum history map](https://egyptianmuseum.org/interactive-map) — successfully opened as contextual research. No historical dates from it are reproduced.

The museum and city garden are separate walking blocks, not a unified ticketed campus. Rosicrucian Park is used as a pause/exit stage only where paths are open. Parking availability and path access are explicitly subject to construction. The guide distinguishes wheelchair access from stroller access and gives an indoor-only weather alternative.
