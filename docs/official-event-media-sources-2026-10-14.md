# Official event images: sources and rights (2026-10-14)

Lane CNT-MEDIA-2. Same rule as the 2026-10-08 batch ([sources 2026-10-08](official-event-media-sources-2026-10-08.md)): an organiser's own image may be shown beside coverage of **that same event**, labelled 官方图 with a link to the official page; the About page carries the takedown contact. On 2026-10-14 21:15 PT the owner approved the download of the usable rows of the CNT-SUPPLY image list and the 25 **yes** rows of the CNT-SUPPLY-2 list (40 rows, 38 distinct files).

Result: **38 of 38 files downloaded, 14 shipped**, 24 files (26 event rows) looked at and not used. The San Jose Veterans Day Parade file shipped in the first version of this batch and was taken out after review on 2026-10-15: it is the MOAA Silicon Valley Chapter's logo share card, not a picture of the parade. Nothing was substituted. No image was generated, and none was edited beyond resizing and flattening transparency onto white.

## How it is wired

Same as 2026-10-08: one record per image in `src/data/official-event-media.json` (English in `official-event-media-en.json`), `OFFICIAL_EVENT_IMAGE_UPDATES` gives each event its `official-<event id>` key, and `getImageProvenance` labels it 官方图 / Official image / 官方圖 because every credit says 官方 / official. The download record of every image (original URL, retrieval time, SHA-256) is in `scripts/data/official-event-media-downloads.json`, outside the bundle: the registry ships in the site's JavaScript with the image registry, and pages never read those three fields (about 1.3 KB gzip in that chunk for the official images). New since WEB-IMAGES: each new key has `coverOk: true` in `src/data/media-rights-overlay.json` (the cover review), and the 480/800/1200 rungs come from `npm run images:variants` ([images.md](images.md)). Photos (`kind: "photo"`) carry a crop focus; flyers, posters and graphic banners (`kind: "poster"`, `fullFrame`) are letterboxed on a blurred copy of themselves and never cropped.

## Rights

Every record uses `rights.basis: "promo-editorial"` with the official event page as `evidenceUrl` and `scope: "same-event coverage only"`: the organiser published the image on its own event page to promote that event, and BAYLINK shows it only beside coverage of the same event, credited and linked. No organiser in this batch publishes an open licence. Do not use these images in ads, share-card promotion, tester recruiting or fundraising.

Notes per image: the Pleasanton photo is from an earlier celebration (the file was uploaded 2025-12, per its URL), the Irvington market photo was uploaded 2019-11, and the Ecology Center photo may show another Berkeley market day; the captions say so. The Castro Valley banner prints "Photos courtesy of Kalie Granier"; the credit repeats it. The Almaden group photo shows performers and students, including children, at a distance, as the library published it. The CHSA roots-seminar flyer prints door fees ($35 early bird through 10/30, $50, $65 at the door) and an itinerary that runs to 5:30 PM; the event's price now follows the flyer through the editorial overlay (ledger: `source-reads-2026-10.md`, CNT-MEDIA-2), so the picture and the 多少钱 box agree. These caption facts have ledger rows there too.

## Download

One plain GET per distinct URL, from the exact og:image URL in the approved lists (no other client, no page scraping), into a folder outside the repository; content type and size checked against the list's HEAD reply. Two needed a second identical GET: SFPL `2026-03/28324.png` failed with a network error once; the Squarespace veterans flyer first answered with its PNG encoding (1,884,816 B, 2.06x the HEAD size) and, asked for WebP, with exactly the 917,066 B image/webp the list's HEAD described, which is the file that was looked at (and not used, see the table).

## Processing

Main file: original width up to 1280 px (never upscaled), sRGB, transparency flattened onto white (4 files), WebP q82, metadata stripped. Rungs: `npm run images:variants`. The tai chi photo's 800 and 1200 rungs exceed the 90/150 KB budgets at the lowest quality step (dense foliage) and are listed in `scripts/data/image-budget-exceptions.json`.
