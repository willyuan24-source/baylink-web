# Regional attraction photographs

Verified 2026-09-09. All images show their named location, not substitute landscapes or AI illustrations. Each of eight guides has a distinct cover and a second view. Lake Merritt is an explicitly permitted reuse of an existing licensed site photograph; its copied files have separate regional keys.

Full images retain source aspect ratios at up to 1400 pixels, with 480-pixel WebP alternatives. Resizing/compression only; no semantic edits. The Tech source itself is a pre-existing cropped derivative. Applicable CC BY-SA terms continue to apply to the redistributed image derivatives. Photographer credit and license links are embedded in the media metadata and should remain visible with images.

The licensing sections of all eight building/street image file pages were separately checked against extmetadata: Berkeley CC BY 2.0; OMCA CC BY 2.0; Stanford CC BY-SA 4.0; Cantor CC BY-SA 3.0; Filoli house CC BY-SA 4.0; Filoli Conservatory CC BY-SA 3.0; The Tech CC BY 2.0; Japantown CC BY-SA 4.0. These are photograph licenses, not public-domain claims about depicted architecture. Cantor uses the photographer’s requested credit wording, © BrokenSphere / Wikimedia Commons.

Capture dates are source metadata, not a claim that photos show present-day conditions. Berkeley’s source summary says 2009, although its Flickr publication metadata has an earlier date; the caption explicitly attributes 2009 to the source page. The Tech date comes from file-page EXIF. Hakone Bridge’s description specifies 2 April 2006; its upload date is separately recorded by Commons.

All 15 new images were visually reviewed in the contact sheet and at 480-pixel display size; Lake Merritt was reviewed separately. No image is labelled as a current event, current bloom, open trail or current business. Muir images are actual Muir Woods. Hakone images are actual Saratoga Hakone, and their captions flag possible pond work. Sausalito hillside inline image is labelled as a broader town-setting view, not the park path.
