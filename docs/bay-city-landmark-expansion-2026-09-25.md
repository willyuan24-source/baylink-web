### Coordinate method details

- Eight BART-related points use the station centroids in BART’s official geographic data: Daly City, South San Francisco, San Bruno, Millbrae, SFO, Milpitas, Hayward and Fremont. Source: [BART geographic data](https://www.bart.gov/schedules/developers/geo), with the [2025-12 station/track/entrance KMZ](https://www.bart.gov/sites/default/files/2025-12/BART-Stations-tracks-entrances-121025.kmz_.zip).
- Menlo Park and Sunnyvale Caltrain coordinates come from the official station pages’ embedded `field_location` data.
- San Bruno City Park and Hayward Japanese Gardens use the embedded coordinates of their official facility pages. Burgess Park’s city page prints its coordinates directly.
- Sunnyvale Heritage Park Museum and Mission Santa Clara use their own official visitor pages’ map markers. The SCU point identifies the Mission/campus visitor area, not a claim of unrestricted campus access.
- Ed R. Levin uses the official county playground directory’s map link for the main park entrance. The location page itself rejected this research client with HTTP 403; the county directory verified the same address and entrance map.
- Fremont Central Park uses the California Division of Boating and Waterways facility-map location for Lake Elizabeth.
- Orange Memorial Park, Twin Pines Park and Santa Clara Central Park use U.S. Census address geocoding of the official venue addresses (Orange Memorial uses its recreation-building access at 781 Tennis Drive). Census results are approximate address-interpolated approaches, not surveyed park boundaries. See [Census Geocoding Services API](https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/Geocoding_Services_API.html).
- SFO is represented as a separate “SFO Airport” area in San Mateo County, rather than assigning it to the City of San Francisco. The airport’s [official About page](https://www.flysfo.com/about) identifies its unincorporated San Mateo County location.

## City and model changes

City recommendations now point to places inside Daly City, South San Francisco, San Bruno, Millbrae, Belmont, Menlo Park, Sunnyvale, Santa Clara, Milpitas, Hayward and Fremont. SFO has its own area label and focus. Existing place IDs were retained.

Authored clay-style geometry adds public-park paths, benches, trees and a pavilion; BART platform/canopy/train variants; an SFO terminal/tower/aircraft silhouette; a Mission-style SCU facade; and a porch for the Sunnyvale heritage house. These are schematic representations, not photogrammetry or claims that every real building detail is reproduced.

The three older “travel stations” now describe in-game journey starting points in a connected Bay Area, with real rail/ferry information linked separately. They no longer tell users to switch between disconnected regional maps.
