## Scope

- Added `sf-school-district-enrollment-guide` and `east-bay-school-district-enrollment-guide` in `guides-schools-sf-east.ts`, exported as `schoolSfEastGuides`.
- Each has 10 official source links, four practical headings, a reusable inquiry template and four checklist items. Neither has a cover field; parent integration supplies educational illustrations.
- Existing guide scan found adult ESL/noncredit learning content, but no dedicated address-to-district/K–12 enrollment guide. These guides cover K–12 operations and separate college admission/campus lookup; they do not repeat adult ESL instruction.

## sf-school-district-enrollment-guide

1. [https://www.sfusd.edu/schools/enroll/apply](https://www.sfusd.edu/schools/enroll/apply) — Opened official page. Current page explicitly separates 2026–27 new students (accepted throughout the year) from 2027–28 applicants. ParentVUE and paper/PDF routes are listed. The guide does not publish deadlines.
2. [https://www.sfusd.edu/schools/enroll/apply/requirements](https://www.sfusd.edu/schools/enroll/apply/requirements) — Opened official page. Official residency and enrollment requirements read. The guide sends document checks and submissions to the district instead of reproducing a potentially incomplete checklist.
3. [https://www.sfusd.edu/schools/enroll/student-assignment-policy/tiebreakers](https://www.sfusd.edu/schools/enroll/student-assignment-policy/tiebreakers) — Opened official page. Current tiebreaker page read. Confirms different grade/program rules and that attendance-area placement is not guaranteed. See inconsistency note below.
4. [https://www.sfusd.edu/schools/directory](https://www.sfusd.edu/schools/directory) — Opened official page. Grade-level school directory read, with School Finder in its navigation. No private, charter or selective program is treated as automatically following a residential assignment.
5. [https://www.sfusd.edu/schools/enroll/discover/sfusd-school-tours](https://www.sfusd.edu/schools/enroll/discover/sfusd-school-tours) — Opened official page. School tour page read; it announces opening October 1, 2026. The guide only directs readers to current booking availability, without inventing a tour slot.
6. [https://www.sfusd.edu/schools/enroll/apply/spring-transfer](https://www.sfusd.edu/schools/enroll/apply/spring-transfer) — Opened official page. Current page read: eligible current SFUSD students only, excluding charter students; one school/pathway; successful assignment automatically replaces the old placement. This consequence is explicit in the guide.
7. [https://www.ccsf.edu/about/our-locations](https://www.ccsf.edu/about/our-locations) — Opened official page. Campus/center directory read, including Ocean Campus, Mission and Chinatown/North Beach. Actual course location remains a class-schedule question.
8. [https://www.ccsf.edu/apply-ccsf/start-here](https://www.ccsf.edu/apply-ccsf/start-here) — Opened official page. Current Start Here page read, announcing a transition to CCCStart beginning September 22. The guide uses this current entry instead of old CCCApply instructions; it does not assume a year for the undated September 22 sentence.
9. [https://future.sfsu.edu/admissions](https://future.sfsu.edu/admissions) — Opened official page. Admissions page read with first-year, transfer, graduate and international paths. No guarantee, test-score claim or acceptance ranking reproduced.
10. [https://future.sfsu.edu/visit](https://future.sfsu.edu/visit) — Opened official page. Visit Campus page read with guided, campus/housing, self-guided and virtual options. Booking and permitted spaces are left to official instructions.

## east-bay-school-district-enrollment-guide

1. [https://www.acoe.org/34593_3](https://www.acoe.org/34593_3) — Opened official page. County directory and school district map link read. Directory includes district contacts and community-college links; county directory is not presented as an enrollment form.
2. [https://www.cccoe.k12.ca.us/Departments--Schools/County-School-Districts/](https://www.cccoe.k12.ca.us/Departments--Schools/County-School-Districts/) — Opened official page. Readable county district directory opened after the older For-Districts navigation target failed. It lists elementary, high-school and unified districts and links the public-school directory.
3. [https://www.ousd.org/enroll/faqs/applying-for-next-year](https://www.ousd.org/enroll/faqs/applying-for-next-year) — Opened official page. OUSD FAQ read: ChooseOUSD filters by grade/address, EnrollWise submission, tours through schools/calendar. Main enrollment page mixes old and current school-year text; no old deadline copied.
4. [https://www.berkeleyschools.net/admissions/](https://www.berkeleyschools.net/admissions/) — Opened official page. Admissions page read with 2026–27 entry, grade-specific information, centralized assignment, materials and school visits. Zone page independently checked; a zone is not a single-school guarantee.
5. [https://fremontusd.aeries.net/enrollment/Default.aspx](https://fremontusd.aeries.net/enrollment/Default.aspx) — Opened official page. Official Aeries portal read. Enrollment account differs from Parent Portal, address verification requires district help if not recognized, and follow-up virtual appointment is required. It still labels 2025–26 as current; the guide expressly asks readers to confirm the correct year before submitting.
6. [https://www.mdusd.org/families/enrollmentservices/enrollnow](https://www.mdusd.org/families/enrollmentservices/enrollnow) — Opened official page. Current Enrollment Center page read, with 2026–27, centralized enrollment, address changes, transfers and School Finder. Finder page independently says the map is a guide and final assignment needs official confirmation.
7. [https://www.srvusd.net/Departments/Educational-Services/Enrollment-Info/Enrollment/](https://www.srvusd.net/Departments/Educational-Services/Enrollment-Info/Enrollment/) — Opened official page. Enrollment page read: 2026–27 open for all grades, locate resident school, prepare documents, apply, and review. Official locator warns that growth/capacity can prevent attendance at the resident school.
8. [https://home.peralta.edu/enroll](https://home.peralta.edu/enroll) — Opened official page. Official page read with all four colleges, their links and the Campus Solutions registration step. Its current account-opening wording differs from older search snippets; the guide follows the opened page and avoids a fixed processing-time promise.
9. [https://admissions.berkeley.edu/apply-to-berkeley/](https://admissions.berkeley.edu/apply-to-berkeley/) — Opened official page. Official undergraduate application page read, with separate first-year and transfer requirements/checklists. No local-residency guarantee or fixed application fee/deadline copied.
10. [https://admissions.berkeley.edu/visit/](https://admissions.berkeley.edu/visit/) — Opened official page. Official visit page read. Campus tours and admissions presentations have separate booking routes; the guide keeps them separate.

## Additional checks and source limitations

- [SFUSD applicant types](https://www.sfusd.edu/schools/enroll/apply/student-needs) was opened for interdistrict procedure and support context. It retains 2024–25 and 2025–26 dates, so those dates are not reused. The guide tells families to confirm permits with both districts.
- [SFUSD attendance areas](https://www.sfusd.edu/attendance-area-schools) was opened but was last updated in 2019. The current tiebreakers page itself has inconsistent middle-school narrative: the table emphasizes feeders, while a later paragraph mentions an attendance-area tiebreaker after saying middle schools lack geographic borders. The guide does not adjudicate that inconsistency or assert a middle-school attendance area. It says to check rules by grade/program with the Enrollment Center.
- [Fremont Unified homepage](https://fremontunified.org/) and [enrollment shortcut](https://fremontunified.org/enroll) returned `Internal Error` to the web tool. ACOE confirms the district's official domain, and the district-branded Aeries portal was fully readable. No claim is based on inaccessible homepage content. The city-hosted district newsletter search result also identifies the `/enroll` shortcut but was not relied on for present dates.
- [CCCOE public-school directory wrapper](https://www.cccoe.k12.ca.us/For-Districts/Public-Schools-Directory/index.html) opened, but its PDF link and older county-district navigation target returned `Internal Error`. The guide instead cites the successfully opened district directory; the full PDF was not reviewed.
- [CCCOE education facts](https://www.cccoe.k12.ca.us/About-CCCOE/About/Education-Facts--Statistics/) was opened to corroborate separate elementary, high-school and unified districts. No statistics or school rankings are included in the guide.
- [Berkeley school zones](https://www.berkeleyschools.net/admissions/find-your-busd-zone/) was opened. Its address/border caveat is reflected by requiring district confirmation rather than treating lookup output as a guarantee.
- [Mt. Diablo School Finder](https://www.mdusd.org/families/enrollmentservices/enrollnow/find-your-school) and [SRVUSD School Site Locator](https://www.srvusd.net/Departments/Educational-Services/Enrollment-Info/School-Site-Locator/index.html) were opened for map/assignment limitations. Actual address searches were not performed.
- [ChooseOUSD](https://www.chooseousd.org/en) was opened; the extractor only exposes a short portal shell. Instructions are grounded in the readable official OUSD FAQ, not an assumed submitted application.
- No enrollment form was submitted; no login, protected student record, live seat count or personal address was accessed. Availability, deadlines, eligibility, grade-specific documents, fees, support and visits remain official-confirmation fields.
