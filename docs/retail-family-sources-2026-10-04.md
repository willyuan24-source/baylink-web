## Verification details and limits

### Michaels

Read the official events calendar and six individual event pages. The publicly served class HTML contains a `__NEXT_DATA__` script with `props.pageProps.posts`; its `scheduleResponseList[].scheduleLocalTime` confirms the **2026** event dates and local times:

- Yarn Wrapped Frame — October 10, 10:00 AM–12:00 PM; ages 4+; listed free.
- Ghost in a Jar — October 11, 1:00–3:00 PM; ages 13+; **buy a jar**, other project supplies provided.
- Halloween Fest — October 17, 10:00 AM–12:00 PM; ages 4+; free crafts, games, giveaways and trick-or-treating.
- Halloween Lanterns — October 18, 1:00–3:00 PM; ages 13+; **buy a jar**, decorations provided.
- Chenille Stem Ghost or Spider — October 24, 10:00 AM–12:00 PM; ages 4+; listed free.
- Last Minute Costume Demo — October 25, 1:00–3:00 PM; ages 13+; free demonstration, not a free costume or kit.

The zero class price in structured data does **not** override the required jar purchase in the description. Both jar events are classified as `purchase`. Default server-side schedules initially list eastern stores; they do not prove available supplies or places at a particular Bay Area store. Every card tells readers to select and confirm their local branch. [The Emeryville official store page](https://locations.michaels.com/ca/emeryville/1538) confirms a Bay Area location at 3991 Hollis St. No unverified reservation requirement or free-material guarantee has been added.
