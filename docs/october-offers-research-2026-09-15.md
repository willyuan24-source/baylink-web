## Source differences and exclusions

- SMCL’s February 2025 promotional article says users must be 14+, but the current detailed FAQ’s “How does Discover & Go Work?” section says at least 16. Published copy uses the more restrictive current detailed FAQ and links it directly. Do not replace this with the City of San Mateo library’s 14+ rule: it is a different library.
- Some third-party Lowe’s guides say October 10. The actual Lowe’s registration and workshop calendar both say October 17, 2026; the website uses October 17.
- OMCA now explicitly includes all special exhibitions on First Sundays. Old PDFs stating a surcharge were not used.
- Bank of America’s roster lists SFMOMA only June–September, SJMA Sundays only and The Tech select weekends. The new October card is specific to CHM and does not imply all Bay Area partners participate on both October dates.
- Bay Area Discovery Museum’s current visitor page shows an annual closure September 14–25 and does not state the historical free-first-Wednesday promotion. That old benefit was not added.
- FAMSF free-Saturday pages returned HTTP 403; they were not added as new primary-verified offers. A library partner page mentions their program, but a fresh new card was unnecessary when stronger sources were available.
- The Home Depot existing October 3 Witch Candy Box card was retained. This check read its free-first-Saturday-at-9-AM rule, while specific project imagery and store selection were verified in the repository’s prior September work; no new October Home Depot project was invented.
- BAMPFA’s official indexed calendar supplied the explicit 2026 October 1 entry, and the official indexed hours page supplied gallery/film distinctions. A later direct fetch returned 403. GGGP’s official indexed pages supplied full current rules; some later direct fetches failed. These read limitations do not imply per-date ticket availability was verified.
