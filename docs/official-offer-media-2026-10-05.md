# Official offer media audit — 2026-10-05

Added 26 verified official media assets and 26 specific offer mappings. Stored assets are resized/compressed copies of actual source pixels; no image generation, invented products, added text, compositing or synthetic people were used. Images wider than 480 pixels have a 480-pixel-wide responsive copy; the two original 375-pixel Peet’s images use a single source candidate (50 WebP files total).

## Selection and provenance

- Prefer the exact activity/product image on the current official source page. Official promotional photography is labelled as promotion rather than a photograph of the upcoming event.
- Lowe’s Firefighting Plane is the same photograph shown in the user’s official-page screenshot. The full original was retrieved from Lowe’s CDN, not cropped out of the screenshot.
- Michaels: each of the six October activities has its own actual project photo. The platform CDN served challenge text for some transformed URLs; the original asset URL (without resizing query) returned a valid image.
- PetSmart’s photograph comes from its brand-issued PR Newswire release (explicit credit: PetSmart). It is mapped to pet trick-or-treating dates, not the separate snakes/spiders educational event.
- Target eos uses the general participating-store asset, not the Westbury-only personalized-gift/latte image. ACOTAR images identify the separately sold book and limited keychain.
- Taco Bell uses the first frame of the GIF currently served by its official offers page. The app controls actual current reward choices.
- Lowe’s Holiday Engine (November 14) and Winter Play Lodge (January 16) use their exact official project photographs. Holiday Trolley Car remains without an assigned photo because a matching official image was not confirmed.
- The IKEA heritage-meal card uses the matching chicken photo. The Tres Leches photo is retained as a documented supporting image for the same offer; it is not counted as a separately illustrated offer.
- Narrow Lowe’s Money Days/MrBeast banners remain intact with fullFrame enabled. Do not crop away their terms or reconstruct an unofficial giveaway poster.
- Copyright remains with each brand/rightsholder; source attribution is not an open-license claim.

2. Apply `officialOfferMediaUpdates[offer.id]` after older per-offer overrides so obsolete AI image keys and image notes are replaced together.
3. Import `official-offer-media-2026-10-en.json` in the English locale dictionary loader. It translates every new alt, caption, credit and short image note.
