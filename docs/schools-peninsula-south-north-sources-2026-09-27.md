# Peninsula, South Bay, and North Bay school guides — source review

Reviewed: 2026-09-27 (America/Los_Angeles).

Scope: three evergreen guides for public K–12 district lookup, enrollment navigation, transfer questions, newcomer support, school visits, and separate college application/visit pathways. No individual residence was queried, no personal student records were collected, and no enrollment application was submitted.

Existing guide data was searched for school/district/enrollment coverage. Existing adult ESL, library, and general newcomer material did not cover these regional K–12 workflows. These guides are additive rather than replacements.

Each guide includes ten official source links. All listed URLs were opened during this review; readable-page versus limited-extraction details are noted below. No selected source relies on a search-only 403 page. Official search excerpts were supplementary navigation evidence, not proof of application acceptance or appointment availability.

## peninsula-school-district-enrollment-guide

- [SMCOE: district and school directory](https://www.smcoe.org/schools/school-districts) — Find county districts, school directories, and school-year calendars.
- [SMFCSD: school locator](https://www.smfcsd.net/district-departments/student-services/enrollment/school-locator) — Address results are preliminary; confirm placement through enrollment.
- [SMFCSD: 2026–27 new-student registration](https://www.smfcsd.net/district-departments/student-services/enrollment/2026-2027-new-student-registration-process) — Check the current-year process, documents, and placement rules.
- [SMUHSD: new-student enrollment](https://www.smuhsd.org/departments/student-services/enroll-a-new-student) — The high-school district separately reviews enrollment and placement.
- [PAUSD: registration and school lookup](https://www.pausd.org/enrollment/registration) — Check district residence before choosing the relevant school year.
- [PAUSD: English learner support](https://www.pausd.org/student-supports/english-learner-program) — Learn about language assessment, English development, and school contacts.
- [College of San Mateo: campus and services](https://collegeofsanmateo.edu/) — The homepage links to campus maps, visits, and student services.
- [College of San Mateo: application entry](https://collegeofsanmateo.edu/apply/) — Choose the official application and registration route for your student type.
- [Stanford: undergraduate applications](https://admission.stanford.edu/apply/) — Distinguish first-year and transfer applications.
- [Stanford: campus and virtual visits](https://admission.stanford.edu/engage/) — Arrange a visit or explore online programs; visiting does not determine admission.

### Verification and editorial limits

- San Mateo County has separate elementary/high-school districts as well as unified districts. SMFCSD/SMUHSD is presented as an example, not a Peninsula-wide assignment rule.
- SMFCSD's 2026–27 page contains a priority period that has already passed. The guide links the current-year process but does not repeat the expired priority cutoff or promise a neighborhood-school seat.
- PAUSD registration also retains historical priority dates. No priority dates, tuition claims, school rankings, or guaranteed language-program placement are reproduced.
- College of San Mateo's homepage was readable and directly links Apply & Enroll, campus maps, and tour scheduling. Its /apply/ and /tour/ destinations returned only a title or an empty extraction. The guide keeps /apply/ as the institution's verified navigation target, uses the readable homepage for campus navigation, and does not derive admissions requirements from the thin page.
- Stanford's Apply and Engage pages eventually opened with short extractions after an initial timeout. Official search results corroborated first-year/transfer application navigation and campus/virtual engagement. No application deadlines or visit-slot availability are claimed.

## south-bay-school-district-enrollment-guide

- [SCCOE: district lookup by address](https://www.sccoe.org/resources/families/Pages/School-District-Locator.aspx) — Identify the district first, then confirm the assigned school with it.
- [SCCOE: public school directory](https://publicschooldirectory.sccoe.org/) — Look up districts, schools, and official contact information.
- [SJUSD: enrollment and school-year locators](https://www.sjusd.org/enrollment) — Separate locators apply to 2026–27 and to 2027–28 onward.
- [Cupertino Union: school locator](https://www.cusdk8.org/registration/locator) — Check preliminary placement, then confirm with the enrollment department.
- [Sunnyvale School District: new-student enrollment](https://www.sesd.org/about-usnew/departments/student-information-enrollment/new-student-registration) — Check enrollment steps for the grade, school year, and available capacity.
- [FUHSD: high-school enrollment and address check](https://www.fuhsd.org/departments/enrollment) — Use the high-school district’s own address tool and enrollment center.
- [San José State: admissions](https://sjsu.edu/admissions/) — Check requirements for first-year, transfer, and other applicant types.
- [San José State: campus visits](https://www.sjsu.edu/soar/campus-tours/index.php) — Explore guided, self-guided, and virtual visits.
- [Foothill College: apply and register](https://foothill.edu/apply-and-register/) — Choose application, advising, and course-registration steps for your student type.
- [Foothill College: admissions office and campus map](https://foothill.edu/apply-and-register/admissions-and-records/index.html) — Check the campus service location and current contact options.

### Verification and editorial limits

- SCCOE's locator directs families toward the county address/parcel lookup and then the district for school placement; it is not itself a guaranteed school assignment.
- SJUSD's opened enrollment page explicitly has different locators for 2026–27 and 2027–28 onward. The guide retains this verified distinction and does not treat future attendance boundaries as current.
- Cupertino Union and Sunnyvale School District are not interchangeable with FUHSD high-school enrollment. The city examples explicitly require address confirmation, rather than claiming that every city address belongs to the named districts.
- Sunnyvale's page includes past 2026–27 initial registration and open-enrollment windows. Those dates are omitted.
- The SJSU campus-tours URL redirects to its current Explore and Visit page. The canonical official incoming link is kept; guided, self-guided, and virtual options were visible.
- Foothill's admissions-office page identifies the campus office and provides a campus-map link. The guide does not promise a guided Foothill tour, a fee waiver, course space, or transfer admission.

## north-bay-school-district-enrollment-guide

- [Marin County: district directory and boundaries](https://www.marinschools.org/districts-schools/districts/marin-county-school-districts) — Look up elementary, high-school, or unified districts by address.
- [Sonoma County: school and district directory](https://www.scoe.org/directory) — The directory links to official district lookup and school information.
- [Sonoma County: attendance and transfer options](https://www.scoe.org/for-families/attendance-options) — Distinguish intradistrict school changes from interdistrict transfer review.
- [Napa County: district entry points](https://napacoe.org/districts/) — Check separate districts including NVUSD, St. Helena, and Calistoga.
- [NVUSD: new-student registration and school assignment](https://www.nvusd.org/enrolling-in-nvusd/school-registration) — Confirm the relevant school year, school assignment, and current system status.
- [College of Marin: applications](https://www1.marin.edu/apply) — Follow the college application route for the student category.
- [College of Marin: visits to both campuses](https://ss.marin.edu/outreach) — Contact the college to arrange a Kentfield or Indian Valley campus tour.
- [Santa Rosa Junior College: admissions and registration](https://admissions.santarosa.edu/) — Find application steps and service locations in Santa Rosa and Petaluma.
- [Santa Rosa Junior College: campus tours](https://studentlife.santarosa.edu/campus-tours) — Prospective students can explore campus through official visit arrangements.
- [Napa Valley College: admissions and campus services](https://www.napavalley.edu/admissions-and-aid/index.html) — Follow application steps and links to the Welcome Center, campus maps, and parking.

### Verification and editorial limits

- Marin's page separates elementary, high-school, and unified districts and links an address/parcel boundary lookup.
- SCOE's directory links the county District and Elected Official Lookup; its attendance-options page distinguishes intradistrict and interdistrict procedures. No countywide acceptance or transportation entitlement is inferred.
- Napa County's district directory includes several districts; NVUSD is not treated as the entire county.
- NVUSD's opened registration page has an October 15, 2026 heading, while its open-enrollment link still names an October–November 2025 window. Its interactive-map notice directs families to School of Residence for greater accuracy. The guide publishes neither window as a currently applicable deadline and directs families to district confirmation. It makes no claim that a registration system is currently unavailable.
- College of Marin's www1.marin.edu/apply was reached through the readable official outreach page and opened successfully; the shorter www.marin.edu/apply attempt failed. The working official URL is used.
- SRJC admissions lists Santa Rosa and Petaluma service locations; its Student Life tour page provides prospective-student campus visits.
- Napa Valley College's admissions page links new-student steps, the Welcome Center, maps, and parking. The guide does not claim all services are available at every campus.

## Shared editorial decisions

- Addresses and student documents are directed only to official district tools and staff. The inquiry template uses school year, grade, and support categories without child name, date of birth, full address, or document numbers.
- Material examples are categories to verify against the applicable district checklist, not a universal required-document list. Families with missing documentation, shared housing, or temporary accommodation are directed to ask about alternatives and assistance.
- Language, learning support, transportation, after-school eligibility, fees, and visits are framed as questions for the responsible institution; no availability or entitlement is assumed.
- City names and real-estate descriptions do not establish either district residence or an individual school assignment. Boundary lookups are followed by district confirmation.
- College applications, course registration, campus visits, fees, and K–12 assignment are separate processes. No college admission, course seat, fee waiver, or transfer outcome is promised.
- Suggested ordering, comparison questions, and checklists are editorial planning advice. No school ranking or comparative quality claim is made.
- The two data files were authored together: every Chinese string has an exact-key English translation after trim/whitespace normalization, including source titles/descriptions, metadata, templates, tips, and calls to action. English values contain no Chinese text.
