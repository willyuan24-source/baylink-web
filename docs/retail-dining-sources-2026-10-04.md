# Retail and dining offer review — 2026-10-04

Review date: 2026-10-04, America/Los_Angeles. Public official pages were reviewed without signing in or claiming an offer. This is evidence of published conditions, not a guarantee that a particular franchise or account will accept a coupon. Store links establish a Bay Area location; the card still requires checking participation.

- `diningRetailUpdates2026` refreshes the existing `roundup-krispy-kreme-welcome-2026` review date/source label. It does not add a second welcome offer.

- No expired event was extrapolated into October 2026. `ongoing` means the official site currently publishes a standing program; it does not override a coupon's individual expiry.
- Purchase classification includes prior spending, tier qualification, buy-one-get-one deals and paid automatic shipments. Only Taco Bell's welcome reward and Habit's registration-day sundae are marked no-purchase.
