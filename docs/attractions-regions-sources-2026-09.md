# Regional attraction editorial sources

Verified 2026-09-09. Eight substantive Chinese guides plus complete English display dictionary. Suggested durations, order, rest stops and community prompts are BAYLINK editorial guidance. ## 从校园钟楼到山中植物园：Berkeley 的两种慢生活

Slug: berkeley-campus-botanical-garden-half-day

Campus outdoor access and garden admission are separated. Garden Tuesday closure, 4 p.m. greenhouse/Redwood Grove closing, $18/$12/$8 age/student tickets and weekday-only H shuttle are checked against the garden pages. The 2021 campus map only supports stable landmarks; current access signs take priority.

- [UC Berkeley：自助校园地图](https://visit.berkeley.edu/sites/default/files/berkeley-self-guided-tour-map-2021.pdf) — Locate Sather Gate and Memorial Glade; use this older map with current access signs.
- [UC Botanical Garden：参观与门票](https://botanicalgarden.berkeley.edu/visit/plan-your-visit/) — Check Tuesday closures, age-based tickets, recommended reservations and earlier greenhouse closing.
- [UC Botanical Garden：上山与停车](https://botanicalgarden.berkeley.edu/visit/plan-your-visit/directions-parking/) — The H shuttle runs on weekdays only; parking costs extra and spaces are not guaranteed.
- [UC Botanical Garden：无障碍说明](https://botanicalgarden.berkeley.edu/visit/accessibility/) — Paths include slopes, gravel and steps; choose a route for your mobility needs.

## Oakland 不必绕湖一整圈：Lake Merritt 与 OMCA 的松弛半日

Slug: oakland-lake-merritt-omca-half-day

Free OMCA garden is distinguished from paid galleries. Current visitor page lists adult all-inclusive $25 and free first-Sunday admission; the guide does not repeat older special-exhibition exclusions. Short lakeside walk returns to the same museum/BART district rather than claiming a full-lake loop.

- [OMCA：开放、门票与到达](https://museumca.org/visit/) — Check tickets, Lake Merritt BART, parking and accessible entrances.
- [OMCA：免费花园](https://museumca.org/on-view/garden/) — The free garden follows museum hours; closures and gatherings have separate rules.
- [Oakland 市府：Lakeside Park](https://www.oaklandca.gov/Community/Parks-Facilities/Parks/Lakeside-Park) — Learn about the lake, paved paths and park facilities; not every attraction is free.

## Stanford 的免费艺术下午：Main Quad、Cantor 与一小段校园散步

Slug: stanford-cantor-campus-art-walk

Cantor is free but currently closed Tuesdays and Wednesdays, not Mondays. Main Quad outdoor walk does not promise access to classrooms/church/tower. Visitor parking is a separate ParkMobile expense; campus self-guided access and museum mobility advice remain distinct.

- [Cantor：参观、开放与无障碍](https://museum.stanford.edu/visit) — Check free admission, Tuesday/Wednesday closures, galleries and accessible entry.
- [Stanford：校园访客信息](https://visit.stanford.edu/) — Self-guided visits differ from admission tours, which require booking and follow an academic schedule.
- [Stanford：停车与付款](https://transportation.stanford.edu/parking-stanford) — Use designated visitor spaces and check ParkMobile instructions and posted hours.
- [Stanford：校园公共艺术地图](https://public-art.stanford.edu/) — Choose a short route by artwork location instead of trying to circle the whole campus.

## Filoli 不只是一张花园照：把老宅、花径和林间留成半天

Slug: filoli-house-garden-day-trip

No fixed admission price is invented: prices and timed arrival are checked for the chosen date. General admission includes listed estate areas, with parking included; special events can differ. Strollers are allowed but wagons and stroller wagons are not. Paths are mixed historic brick/gravel/grass with alternative access described by Filoli.

- [Filoli：日期、门票与开放时间](https://filoli.org/visit/opening-times/) — Choose an actual visit date and entry time; general admission differs from special evening events.
- [Filoli：交通与停车](https://filoli.org/visit/directions-parking/) — Parking is included; overflow lots may be used on busy days.
- [Filoli：无障碍参观](https://filoli.org/visit/accessibility/) — Stairless maps are available, but brick, grass and gravel can affect access.
- [Filoli：推车及访客常见问题](https://filoli.org/visit/faqs/visit-faqs/wagons-strollers/) — Check entry windows, food rules and the distinction between strollers and stroller wagons.

## San Jose 一天，两种节奏：The Tech 动手玩，再去 Japantown 散步

Slug: san-jose-tech-japantown-day-trip

The Tech is a paid indoor block and Japantown a separate neighborhood block, not one continuous doorway-to-doorway attraction. Current $38 adult/$28 child-student-senior admission includes one educational IMAX. $5 validated garage rate is limited to the two named garages and excludes special-event rate days. No current shops, meals or event offers are invented.

- [The Tech：门票与每日安排](https://www.thetech.org/visit/) — General admission includes one educational IMAX film; hours vary by season and date.
- [The Tech：到达与停车验证](https://www.thetech.org/visit/directions-parking/) — Check the two participating garages and special-event parking exceptions.
- [Japantown 商业协会：位置与交通](https://www.japantownsanjose.org/where-is-jtown) — Jackson and North Fifth form the central area; the light-rail stop is not at every shop’s door.
- [The Tech：感官参观资源](https://www.thetech.org/explore/sensory-resources/) — Preview sound, lighting and interactive spaces through sensory guides and social stories.

## Saratoga 山脚的安静下午：Hakone 的池桥、竹影与慢步

Slug: hakone-gardens-saratoga-half-day

Seasonal hours quote only March 9–October 31, 2026, with separate weekday/weekend last-entry times. First-Tuesday resident free admission is Saratoga only with government photo ID/local address, not Santa Clara County. Historical terrain is not fully accessible. Pond renovation is an active project to check, not an asserted closure on the visit date.

- [Hakone：访客信息与园区地图](https://www.hakone.com/visitor-info) — Check age-based prices, seasonal hours, resident discounts and uneven paths.
- [Hakone：庭园地图入口](https://www.hakone.com/gardens) — Use the official map without assuming every hillside area is accessible.
- [Hakone：池塘整修项目](https://www.hakone.com/koi-pond-renovation) — The project concerns the pond and accessibility improvements; reference photos do not guarantee current conditions.
- [Hakone：当天公告](https://www.hakone.com/) — Special events may change accessible areas and entry times.

## 第一次去 Muir Woods：先订停车，再用短环线走进红木林

Slug: muir-woods-reservation-day-trip

NPS admission $15 for ages 16+ and free ages 15-and-under remains separate from all-vehicle parking and all-rider shuttle reservations. Authorized concessioner lists standard parking $10 and adult round-trip shuttle $4; child shuttle fare is free but reserved. No cell/Wi-Fi and departure planning are explicit. Bridge 2/3 routes are options, not sequential compulsory loops; the west side between bridges 2 and 3 can be rougher.

- [NPS：Muir Woods 费用与通票](https://www.nps.gov/muwo/planyourvisit/fees.htm) — Standard admission is $15 for ages 16+ and does not include parking or shuttle reservations.
- [官方授权：停车与接驳预约](https://gomuirwoods.com/) — Check dates, standard-vehicle parking and shuttle tickets before reaching the valley without reception.
- [NPS：步道与短环线](https://www.nps.gov/muwo/planyourvisit/hike.htm) — Bridge 2, 3 and 4 routes differ in length; uphill extensions are not equivalent to the main trail.
- [NPS：无障碍与无信号准备](https://www.nps.gov/muwo/planyourvisit/accessibility.htm) — Some main-trail surfaces have roots and cracks; ask about current conditions.

## 坐船去 Sausalito 发半天呆：海滨、长椅和一段小镇散步

Slug: sausalito-waterfront-ferry-half-day

Golden Gate Ferry route uses the Ferry Building, not another operator at Pier 41. Fares are explicitly one-way: adult Clipper/contactless $8.50, adult paper $14, youth 5–18 and senior65+ $7. Free age<=4 limit is two children per full-fare adult. The route stays in Sausalito and returns to the same pier; it does not combine Tiburon or Muir Woods. Accessible preboarding requires at least20 minutes and notifying staff.

- [Golden Gate Ferry：现行时刻表](https://www.goldengate.org/ferry/schedules-maps/) — Choose weekday or weekend schedules and check service alerts.
- [Golden Gate Ferry：单程票价与支付](https://www.goldengate.org/ferry/ferry-fares-payment/) — Clipper/contactless and paper fares differ; each rider needs their own payment credential.
- [Golden Gate Ferry：无障碍登船](https://www.goldengate.org/ferry/accessibility/) — Passengers can request preboarding from terminal staff and should arrive at least 20 minutes early.
- [Sausalito 市府：Gabrielson Park](https://www.sausalito.gov/Home/Components/FacilityDirectory/FacilityDirectory/2/690) — The park beside the pier has lawns, benches and bay views; events may affect access.
